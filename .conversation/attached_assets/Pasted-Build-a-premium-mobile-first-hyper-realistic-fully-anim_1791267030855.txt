Build a premium, mobile-first, hyper-realistic, fully animated website for Café GLAD in Aarhus, Denmark.

GOAL
The moment someone opens the site they should feel hungry and be one tap away from ordering. Every screen has one obvious next action: order takeaway. Secondary goals: café visits, catering enquiries, Google discoverability, and turning Instagram/Facebook visitors into customers.

It must feel like a modern Danish organic café with real personality: warm, human, handmade. Not a template, not corporate, not overly luxurious.

- Hover/tap: cards lift and tilt slightly, images zoom inside their frame, buttons have a soft orange fill sweep.
- Hand-drawn line illustrations (leaves, steam, bicycle) that draw themselves as they enter view.
- Bicycle delivery section: a small animated bicycle rides across the screen on scroll.
- Marquee strip of "Økologisk • Hjemmelavet • Fra bunden • Aarhus".
- Micro-interactions: add-to-order button ripples, a cart badge bounces, success states animate.
- Only animate transform and opacity, lazy-load media, and respect prefers-reduced-motion (disable all non-essential motion).

6. Sticky order button that follows the visitor on every screen, plus a mobile bottom bar: BESTIL / MENU / RING / FIND VEJ.
